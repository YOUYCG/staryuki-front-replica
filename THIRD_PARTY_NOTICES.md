## Generated sound effects

The WAV files under `dist/audio/` are locally generated by
`generate-sounds.mjs`. They are not recordings downloaded from the original
site. Public launcher SFX event names and timing were used as behavioral
references; original audio retrieval returned HTTP 502 on 2026-10-20.

The repository's MIT license applies to the generator and these generated
sound effects.
